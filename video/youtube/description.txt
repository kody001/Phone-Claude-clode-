Florida had the highest foreclosure rate in America in the first half of 2026. Yet the statewide median price of a single-family home is still going up. So is Florida's housing market crashing, or is it fine?

The answer is both. Florida is cracking over the price of KEEPING a home: insurance bills, condo assessments after the Surfside collapse, stricter Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac condo rules, and buyers who bought near the 2022 peak with small down payments. Here's what happened, why Southwest Florida got hit hardest, and why it matters everywhere else.
