TITLE
The SpaceX IPO Was Never About Space

DESCRIPTION
SpaceX just pulled off the biggest IPO in history: $75 billion raised, a $2 trillion valuation on day one, and Elon Musk became the first trillionaire. But SpaceX's own filing says 93% of the market it's chasing is AI. Space is about 1% of the pitch.

I break down what investors actually bought: xAI's losses, the $20B bridge loan, the Grok subscriptions banks had to buy, the Nasdaq rule that pushed SpaceX into your index fund in 15 trading days, the $60B Cursor deal paid in stock, and why SpaceX's biggest business is now renting out computers on Earth.

If you own a Nasdaq index fund, you probably own SpaceX right now. Even if you never clicked buy.
